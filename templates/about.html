About The AI Times

The AI Times is a daily-updated hub for artificial intelligence news — research breakthroughs, product launches, startups, and industry shifts, curated in one place so you don't have to chase a dozen sources.

Why we built this

AI moves fast, and news about it is scattered across research papers, company blogs, and tech journalism. The AI Times pulls it together and keeps it organized by category, source, and recency.

What you'll find here

  • Latest and archived AI news in one searchable place
  • Coverage across research, products, startups, and policy
  • Category filters for major labs like OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, and Meta

This project

This site is a student-built project focused on aggregating AI news from multiple sources and making it easy to browse and stay updated.
